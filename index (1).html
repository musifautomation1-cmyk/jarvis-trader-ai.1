Your personal rule engine

XAUUSD strategy check

30-minute swing → 75-point inward levels → 50% midpoint → 5-minute confirmation.

For XAUUSD quoted to 2 decimals, this prototype treats 75 points as 0.75 in price (75 × 0.01). Confirm this matches your exact Exness symbol specification before relying on it.
Rule reminders
• Use a clearly identified 30M swing high and swing low.
• Mark 75 points inward from both ends.
• Midpoint = (swing high + swing low) ÷ 2.
• The app calculates levels; it does not decide that a trade is valid without your full entry rules.
Strategy 02 · Candlestick patterns

Select the candle pattern you see on your chart. Jarvis will explain its usual bias and ask you to check context. This is a manual learning/checking tool, not automatic chart recognition.

Important: A candlestick pattern alone is not an entry signal. The same pattern can fail, especially in choppy markets. Wait for a closed candle, location, structure confirmation and a planned stop-loss. Do not treat this checklist as a tested profitable strategy.
